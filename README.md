# 🔐 Security+ Cybersecurity Portfolio

**Focus:** CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) · Defensive cybersecurity · Practical lab evidence  
**Status:** In development · **Started:** November 2026

Welcome! This repository tracks my progress applying cybersecurity concepts to realistic, **fictional business scenarios** through an authorized home lab. I am building practical skills in networking, vulnerability management, security operations, awareness, and risk management. My QA background informs how I design validation steps, record evidence, and report findings.

> **Evidence policy:** Plans and templates are not completed lab work. Projects remain **Not started** until I perform and document them. Any example data will be clearly labeled as simulated.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Design and validate secure network controls.
- Identify vulnerabilities in a controlled lab and verify remediation.
- Interpret event logs and document defensible investigation findings.
- Communicate effective employee security guidance.
- Assess business risk and prepare incident response documentation.
- Apply QA habits: repeatable test steps, expected vs. observed outcomes, and evidence-based reporting.

## 🛠️ How I document a finished lab

Each project records: business problem, scope and authorization, lab setup, tools, implementation, test cases, actual results, sanitized screenshots/logs, findings, remediation, retest evidence, and a reflection on what I learned. See [documentation standards](DOCUMENTATION-STANDARDS.md).

## 🔒 Ethics and lab safety

All hands-on security testing is limited to systems I own or am expressly authorized to test. I will not scan third-party networks, expose real credentials or private user data, or publish secrets. Screenshots and logs will be sanitized. Controlled demonstrations will be clearly labeled.

## 🚀 Career direction

This portfolio aims to support learning and discussion for junior cybersecurity analyst, SOC analyst, IT security, and security-focused QA roles. The strongest proof will be **completed labs with verifiable test outcomes**, not a checklist alone.
